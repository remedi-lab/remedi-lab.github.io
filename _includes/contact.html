Work with us!

We are always excited to collaborate with passionate students, clinical practitioners, and researchers interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence, statistics, and mental healthcare.

  • I'm a USI Master's student

    Master Theses & Semester Projects

    Interested in doing your Master's thesis or semester project with the REMEDI Lab? We offer projects covering clinical natural language processing, explainable machine learning, multimodal psychiatric data analysis, and differential privacy.

    Please reach out to our heads or PhD students by email with your CV and academic transcripts, including [Master Project - REMEDI] in your subject line.

  • I'm looking for a PhD position

    Doctoral Opportunities in Computational Medicine & AI

    We welcome prospective PhD students with strong backgrounds in machine learning, statistics, NLP, or computational biomedicine. PhD candidates in the REMEDI Lab are enrolled through USI's doctoral programs, including the PhD in Computational Biomedicine and the Faculty of Informatics.

    If you are interested in joining our group, please send an inquiry with your CV, a statement of research interests, and representative publications/code to our lab heads.

  • Postdoctoral & Visiting Researchers

    We actively collaborate with international partners and support postdocs applying for competitive funding schemes (such as SNSF Swiss Postdoctoral Fellowships, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, and bilateral exchange programs). If you would like to visit or propose a joint grant, please contact us directly.
